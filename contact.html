FAQ

Questions parents often ask.

Can't find your answer? Call 99727 76012 | 91879 63957.

Where is Kiddos Paradise located?+

On 20th Cross Road, opposite St. John the Baptist Church, in CHBS Layout, MC Layout, Vijayanagar, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560040.

How can I contact the school?+

Call 99727 76012 or 91879 63957, email kiddosparadise19@gmail.com, or use the enquiry form on this page.

What programs are available?+

Toddler, Playgroup, Nursery, LKG, UKG, Daycare, Tuition Classes, Summer/Holiday Camp, Kiddos Art Centre and Evening Activities. Please confirm current availability with the school.

What is the school's teaching approach?+

Reggio Emilia and Montessori-inspired progressive learning, with emphasis on exploration, hands-on experiences and child development.

What are the class timings?+

Timings vary by program — see the program comparison or call the school to confirm the current schedule.

Is daycare available?+

Yes, for ages 1–10, from 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM. Contact the school to confirm current days, availability and arrangements.

Do you provide food?+

Food and snacks are provided for daycare. Please check meal arrangements for other programs directly with the school.

Do you offer tuition?+

Yes — CBSE and ICSE tuition from 3:00 PM to 6:00 PM in small batches of 3–4 students. Please ask about supported grades and subjects.

What are the fees?+

Please contact the school for the current fee structure and any applicable offers.

How do I enrol my child?+

Call the school or submit the enquiry form. The team can explain eligibility, availability, documents and next steps. See Admissions.

Is transport available?+

Transport is available. Please ask the school about service areas, routes, timings and charges.

Can I visit the school?+

Yes — contact the school to ask about visit availability and arrange a convenient time.
