## 1. Project overview

Kiddos Paradise wants to replace its old website, whose hosting is
suspended, with a clear informational website that turns local parents
in Vijayanagar, Bengaluru into enquiries for preschool admissions,
daycare and tuition.

### Primary goals

-   Help parents quickly understand the school, learning approach,
    programs, age ranges and timings.
-   Make the Vijayanagar location and both contact numbers prominent.
-   Generate enquiries through phone calls, WhatsApp and an admission
    enquiry form.
-   Build trust with authentic school photographs, the Director's
    introduction, parent reviews and clear practical information.
-   Work especially well on mobile devices, including visitors arriving
    from Meta ads.

### Audience

-   Parents and caregivers of children aged approximately 1--12 years,
    depending on the program.
-   Families in Vijayanagar and nearby Bengaluru neighbourhoods.
-   Parents looking for preschool, daycare, CBSE/ICSE tuition, phonics,
    art activities and holiday programs.

### Success actions

1.  Tap to call either school number.
2.  Start a WhatsApp conversation once the correct WhatsApp number is
    confirmed.
3.  Submit an admission enquiry.
4.  Open directions in Google Maps.
5.  Browse programs and request a school visit.

## 3. Brand and logo direction

### Logo

Use the supplied Kiddos Paradise logo file: -
`Kiddos Paradise - Logo(20261026-061251).png` - Keep the logo artwork,
colours, proportions and lettering unchanged. - Do not redraw, recolour,
crop off parts, stretch or add effects to the logo. - Use a transparent
version if supplied. The provided preview appears to have a black
background; inspect the actual file before using it on a light website.
If the black is part of the image rather than transparency, request a
transparent PNG/SVG from the client instead of removing it without
approval.

### Visual direction

A warm, playful, polished early-learning aesthetic: welcoming rather
than overly childish; plenty of whitespace; rounded cards; friendly
illustrations used sparingly; authentic photographs; accessible contrast
and clear buttons.

### Palette sampled/derived from the supplied logo

Use these as approximate interface colours, not as permission to modify
the logo: - Warm yellow: `#F6C928` - Teal: `#4CB2B4` - Leaf green:
`#A6C83A` - Coral red: `#E73532` - Deep tree-brown: `#7E2A1D` - Cream
background: `#FFF9EE` - Dark body text: `#303238` - White: `#FFFFFF`

Confirm exact brand colours if the client has a brand guide.

### Typography

-   Headings: Nunito or Poppins, rounded and friendly.
-   Body: Inter or Open Sans for readability.
-   Use consistent heading hierarchy and avoid decorative fonts for long
    copy.

### Image direction

Use real, school-owned photos wherever possible. Required photo set: -
Building/front entrance: 2--3. - Classrooms: 5--10. - Play area: 3--5. -
Activities: 8--15. - Events: 5--10. - Director portrait: 1. - Optional
teacher portraits and a 1--2 minute landscape school-tour video.

**Child privacy:** Obtain written parent/guardian consent for each
identifiable child shown. Do not publish photos of children without
consent. Until real photos are supplied, use neutral placeholders during
design and clearly replace them before launch. Do not imply
stock/generated images depict actual Kiddos Paradise students or
facilities.
