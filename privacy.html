Privacy Policy

How Kiddos Paradise uses the information you share through this website.

Draft for school / legal review before launch.

What we collect

When you submit an admission enquiry, we collect the details you enter: parent/guardian name, phone number, your child's age, the program you're interested in, an optional preferred start date and any message you choose to add.

Please don't include medical or other sensitive information about your child in the enquiry form. The school will ask for what's needed during the admission process.

How we use it

We use these details only to respond to your enquiry — for example, to call you back, share program availability and fee information, or arrange a school visit.

We do not sell your information or share it with advertisers.

Who receives it

Enquiries are delivered to the school at kiddosparadise19@gmail.com and, where set up, to a secure enquiry list managed by the school. Only school staff responsible for admissions can access them.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to handle your enquiry and any resulting admission, and delete them when they are no longer needed.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete your enquiry details at any time by emailing kiddosparadise19@gmail.com or calling 99727 76012 | 91879 63957.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not currently use analytics or advertising cookies. If that changes, this policy will be updated first.
