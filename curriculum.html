Our approach to curriculum

Planned experiences that follow children's interests.

Our curriculum blends Montessori materials, Reggio-inspired projects and play-based progressive learning. Rather than a fixed set of worksheets, children move through carefully planned experiences that build skills step by step, while following their natural interests.

What children learn

Seven areas of learning.

🥄1

Practical life

Pouring, spooning, buttoning, sweeping, food preparation, caring for plants. Builds independence, concentration, coordination and confidence.

🖐️2

Sensorial exploration

Touch, sound, colour, shape, size and texture activities. Sharpens observation and prepares the mind for maths and language.

🔤3

Language and literacy

Stories, rhymes, conversation, vocabulary, phonics, letter formation, early reading and writing. Children first speak, listen and play with sounds, then move to reading and writing.

🔢4

Numeracy and logical thinking

Counting with real objects, number recognition, patterns, sorting, shapes, measurement, early addition and subtraction using hands-on materials.

🌍5

Understanding the world

Nature, seasons, animals, science experiments, community helpers, geography, cultures and festivals.

🎨6

Creative expression

Art, craft, clay, music, movement, drama and storytelling. Children are encouraged to express their own ideas, not copy a model.

🤸7

Social, emotional and physical development

Sharing, taking turns, understanding feelings, resolving conflicts, and building gross and fine motor skills through outdoor play and movement.

Programs by age

Step by step, stage by stage.

🌱1–2 years

Toddler Program

A gentle first step into group learning. Sensory play, songs, picture books, movement, simple self-care and separation support.

Goal: comfort, trust, first words and early exploration.
🧸2–3 years

Playgroup

Sensory bins, art, stories, rhymes, practical life, building and pretend play.

Goal: language growth, social skills, independence and curiosity.
🎨3–4.5 years

Nursery

Pre-writing patterns, letter sounds, number sense, creative projects and group activities.

Goal: early literacy and numeracy through play, with growing focus and confidence.
🔤4.5–5.5 years

LKG

Phonics, blending sounds, early reading, counting and number operations, thematic inquiry, show-and-tell.

Goal: strong foundations and confident communication.
🚀5.5–6.5 years

UKG

Reading fluency, sentence writing, logical reasoning, problem-solving, projects and presentations.

Goal: school readiness, self-expression and independent thinking.

Preschool routine

A typical day (preschool).

  1. Arrival and greeting

    A warm welcome and settling-in

  2. Circle time

    Songs, stories, calendar and conversation

  3. Work time

    Child-chosen activities and guided lessons

  4. Snack time

    Practising manners and independence

  5. Outdoor play

    Movement, games and nature exploration

  6. Creative time

    Art, music, drama or project work

  7. Story and reflection

    Winding down before going home

How we track progress

We observe each child daily and track their growth in skills, interests and confidence. Parents receive progress updates so you can see how your child is developing, not just a grade or number.

After UKG

Ready for primary school.

By the time children complete UKG, they are expected to:

  • Read simple sentences
  • Write letters and short words
  • Count and do basic number work
  • Express their ideas
  • Follow instructions
  • Work independently and cooperate with others

Most importantly, they love learning. ♥
