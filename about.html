About Kiddos Paradise

Helping little minds explore, create and grow.

Kiddos Paradise is an early childhood education centre in Vijayanagar, Bengaluru, supporting children through progressive, Reggio Emilia and Montessori-inspired learning experiences.

Our story

Growing with children since 2013.

Kiddos Paradise began in 2013 with a focus on supporting children's early learning and development. Over the years, the centre's program offering has included preschool learning, daycare, tuition and creative enrichment opportunities for children at different stages.

Our goal is to give children opportunities to explore, ask questions, express themselves and develop the skills they need for the next stage of learning.

Our vision

To nurture curious, confident and compassionate learners who are ready to explore the world, express their ideas and continue learning throughout life.

Our mission

  • Provide meaningful, age-appropriate learning experiences.
  • Encourage creativity, curiosity, communication and independence.
  • Support social, emotional, physical and cognitive development.
  • Create a welcoming partnership between educators and families.
  • Help children build a positive foundation for future learning.
Our learning philosophy

Learning through curiosity, creativity and discovery.

Reggio Emilia and Montessori-inspired ideas inform our focus on exploration, hands-on learning, meaningful materials, choice, creativity and growing independence. Educators guide children through activities and conversations that support their development.

We aim to make learning meaningful, engaging and connected to children's interests — not limited to memorising facts.
