About Kiddos Paradise

A happy place for little explorers.

Our story

Kiddos Paradise began in 2013 in Vijayanagar, Bengaluru, with a simple belief: young children learn best when they are curious, comfortable and trusted to explore. What started as a small neighbourhood preschool has grown into a learning space for children from 1 to 12 years, with preschool, daycare, tuition and enrichment classes under one roof.

Our philosophy

Montessori, Reggio Emilia and progressive learning.

We are inspired by two of the world’s most respected approaches to early childhood education, along with progressive learning practices.

🧩

From Montessori

We prepare the classroom so children can choose their own work, practise real-life skills and learn at their own pace. Child-sized furniture, open shelves and hands-on materials help children become independent and focused.

🎨

From Reggio Emilia

We see every child as capable and full of ideas. Learning grows from children’s questions and interests, through projects, art, conversation and exploration. Children’s work is displayed and discussed, so they feel their ideas matter.

💡

Progressive learning

We focus on understanding, not memorising. Children learn through doing, asking, creating and solving problems, while still building strong foundations in reading, numbers and communication.

In our classrooms

What this looks like in our classrooms.

  • Children choose activities and work independently during uninterrupted “work time”
  • Teachers observe, guide and ask questions instead of only giving instructions
  • Learning is hands-on: sand, water, clay, blocks, natural materials, art and stories
  • Children help with everyday tasks like pouring, serving snacks and tidying up
  • Themes and projects follow children’s interests, such as animals, seasons, festivals and community helpers
  • Small group and one-to-one attention so every child is seen and heard
