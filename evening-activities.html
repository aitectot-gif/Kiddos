Evening enrichment

Beyond the classroom: evening enrichment at Kiddos Paradise.

🎉 Open for outsiders as well!

Our evening programs give children the chance to build new skills, discover talents and grow in confidence. Each class is led by a dedicated instructor and designed to be fun, structured and age-appropriate. Open to Kiddos Paradise students and children from the neighbourhood and other schools.

1 · Creative Kiddos Art Centre

Art Classes

Children explore drawing, painting, colour mixing, clay, collage, craft and mixed media. The focus is on imagination and technique, not copying.

Children build: fine motor control, creativity, patience, confidence and the joy of making things.

  • 🎤
  • Ages5 years and above
  • ScheduleEvening class
2 · Confidence on stage

Public Speaking

Through show-and-tell, storytelling, rhymes, role-play, debates and speeches, children learn to speak clearly and confidently in front of others.

Children build: vocabulary, voice and body language, listening skills, stage confidence and the courage to share ideas.

  • 💃
  • Ages3 to 15
  • ScheduleEvening class
  • StyleAsk the school
3 · Rhythm and movement

Dance

A joyful class combining rhythm, movement and expression, with performance opportunities during school events.

Children build: coordination, balance, flexibility, rhythm, fitness and self-expression.

Confirm: dance style (e.g. freestyle, classical, Bollywood).

  • 🔤
  • Ages3 to 12
  • Schedule3 days a week
4 · Sounds to reading

Phonics

A structured, playful program teaching letter sounds, blending, sight words and early reading using games, songs, flashcards and activities.

Children build: clear pronunciation, reading readiness, spelling skills and a love of books.

  • 📚
  • Ages3–12 years
  • Timing3:00 PM–6:00 PM
  • Batch sizeAbout 3–4 students
  • BoardsCBSE & ICSE
5 · Academic support

Tuition Classes (CBSE and ICSE)

Small-batch academic support for school learning, homework help, concept clarity and exam preparation, for CBSE and ICSE students.

Children build: strong concepts, study habits, confidence in subjects and personal attention that large classes cannot offer.

Why choose us

Why choose our evening programs?

  • Small groups with individual attention
  • Experienced, caring instructors
  • Safe, familiar environment
  • Skill-building in a fun, no-pressure way
  • Showcase opportunities, such as art exhibitions, performances and speech days
