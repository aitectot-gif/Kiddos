How it works

Admission in five simple steps.

  1. Contact the school

    Call either number or send an enquiry with your child's age and the program of interest.

  2. Discuss the program

    Confirm eligibility, schedule, availability, fees and any specific requirements.

  3. Arrange a school visit

    Visit the centre and discuss your child's needs with the school team. Please confirm a visit time before travelling.

  4. Complete registration

    Obtain the current admission form and follow the school's instructions.

  5. Confirm the start date

    Finalise documents, payment and joining details directly with the school.

Documents usually needed

  • Child's birth certificate or other accepted age proof.
  • Parent/guardian contact details.
  • Child's recent photograph, if required.
  • Medical or allergy information relevant to your child's care.
  • Previous school records, for tuition or transfer cases.
  • Any other documents the school specifically requests.

Provisional list — school to confirm its official checklist.
