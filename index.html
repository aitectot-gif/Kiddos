Our teaching approach

Learning through curiosity, creativity and discovery.

Our teaching approach draws inspiration from Reggio Emilia and Montessori principles, with an emphasis on progressive learning. Children are encouraged to explore, practise, make choices, express ideas and learn through purposeful activities.

In everyday learning, this can include guided play, hands-on materials, creative projects, early literacy and numeracy activities, conversation, observation and opportunities to practise independence. We aim to make learning meaningful, engaging and connected to children's interests — not limited to memorising facts.

More questions ↗
What age groups does Kiddos Paradise serve?+

Our programs cover children from 1 to 12 years, depending on the program. Please contact us to confirm the best fit for your child's age.

What are the preschool timings?+

Program timings vary. Classes start at 9:30 AM, with individual programs ending between 12:00 PM and 1:30 PM.

Is daycare available?+

Yes. Daycare runs from 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM for children aged 1–10. Contact the school to confirm days, availability and arrangements.

Do you offer tuition classes?+

Yes. We offer CBSE and ICSE tuition from 3:00 PM to 6:00 PM in small batches. Please check age and grade eligibility with the school.

How can I find out about fees?+

Please call the school for current fees, availability and program details.

How do I enquire about admission?+

Call 99727 76012 or 91879 63957, or use the admission enquiry form.
