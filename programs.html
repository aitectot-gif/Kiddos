Toddler Program

A gentle introduction to early learning for toddlers. Through age-appropriate play, sensory exploration, movement, simple creative experiences and interaction with familiar routines, children can begin exploring their environment and developing early communication and social skills.

Playgroup

Playgroup offers a welcoming first step into group learning. Play-based progressive experiences encourage children to explore materials, engage their senses, practise communication, build early relationships and develop confidence with daily routines.

Activities can include guided play, stories, songs, movement, creative expression and early discovery experiences.

  • 🎨
  • Age3–4.5 years
  • Timing9:30 AM–1:00 PM
  • Seats15

Nursery

Nursery supports children's growing language, thinking, creative expression and independence. Hands-on activities and guided play introduce early literacy and numeracy while encouraging children to ask questions, share ideas, explore materials and learn alongside others.

Learning can include stories, vocabulary, sound awareness, number concepts, art, sensory play and practical activities suited to children's development.

  • 🔤
  • Age4.5–5.5 years
  • Timing9:30 AM–1:30 PM
  • Seats15

Lower Kindergarten (LKG)

The LKG program builds foundations for more structured learning through interactive phonics, early mathematics and thematic inquiry. Children practise recognising patterns, expressing ideas, listening, following instructions and exploring concepts through meaningful activities.

A balance of guided learning and hands-on experiences helps develop confidence, curiosity and readiness for the next stage.

  • 🚀
  • Age5.5–6.5 years
  • Timing9:30 AM–1:30 PM
  • Seats15

Upper Kindergarten (UKG)

UKG supports school readiness by helping children develop early reading fluency, logical reasoning, self-expression and problem-solving skills. Learning experiences encourage children to communicate clearly, explore ideas, build independence and apply foundational literacy and numeracy concepts.

The aim is to support a confident transition to formal schooling without unnecessary pressure.

  • 🏡
  • Age1–10 years
  • Timing9:00 AM–7:00 PM
  • MealsFood & snacks provided
Daycare

Care, comfort and play — while you're at work.

Daycare offers a home-like setting for children who need care beyond preschool or school hours — a "second home", with food and snacks provided. A daycare day can include supervised play, quiet time, age-appropriate activities and opportunities to socialise.
